## Visual design

An app built only for readability looks like every other generated app: the starter's blue, raw lists, dark in a dark browser. Give it a deliberate look, with palette objects and styles only (no `UICustom`, no inline style).

- **Choose the tokens before the pages.** Right after the starter import, call `ngx-theme-apply` once: the user's brand color as `primary` when they gave one (a logo, a website, a charter), and the preset that fits the app: `clean` (the default, and the right one for most business apps: indigo on white cards over a light grey page, white toolbar), `fresh` (teal-green: field, health, education), `warm` (terracotta: retail, hospitality, events), `institutional` (only when the user wants an administrative, public-service look: deep blue-grey, colored toolbar, small radius), `modern` (violet, rounded), `dense` (back-office screens full of tables: compact). Give `appName` for the menu header. It writes the app-level `UIStyle` `AppTheme` and keeps the starter's `LightTheme`, `AutoDarkTheme` and `TailwindTheme` underneath. `ngx-page-create` takes the same tokens as `theme`.
- **Never leave the default blue by accident.** While an app still has the starter's colors, `ngx-page-create` applies the `clean` preset and says so in its warnings. `project-lint` reports `NGX-DEFAULT-THEME` for an app that kept the starter's colors.
- **App shell.** From 992 px the menu stays open beside the pages (`sideMenu`, default `lg`; `never` keeps the menu button only), headed by `appName` in the brand color; a sign-in or logout page shows no menu beside it. Pages behind the sign-in (`authRedirect`) show who is signed in on the right of the top bar (the name the sign-in answer gives, else the identifier typed; its role when the answer has one) and a sign-out button once the `logout` page exists. Each menu page has an icon (`icon`, the base ionicons name such as `people`). The toolbar title hides when the page shows its own title.
- **Light by default for a business app**; `mode: "dark"` only when the user asks for it, `"auto"` when the app must follow the device. The dark palette is always written and its contrast is checked.
- **Page header.** Every page opens with its title, a `subtitle` (a text, a value of the data, or `{count: true, suffix: " deals"}`) and its `actions` on the right: the first is the main one (filled), the others outlined. Forms keep their buttons in a bar at the bottom (`actionsAt`).
- **Dashboard.** KPI cards: label above, figure, a `hint` line of context (`{field:"counters.openCount", suffix:" open deals"}`), an accent (`color`, else the words of the label); an icon only when `icon` is given. Sections are cards, two by two on a wide screen, a table across (`wide: true` forces it). A distribution (by stage, by status, by month) is a `chart: "bars"` section: `{heading, items, badge or label, value, note}`, each bar as long as its share of the largest.
- **Lists and tables.** A list whose rows carry more than three values gets `list.columns`: a header row, compact zebra rows, numbers right-aligned in tabular figures, dates in the app's language (`format: "date"`, or a field named as a date), status badges whose color says the status (`badge.labels`; done, won or valid: success, late, lost or refused: danger, waiting: warning). Filters sit in one bar above, the search taking the room left. Every empty state has an icon (`emptyIcon`) and a sentence; a spinner shows while the data loads.
- **Details and forms.** A record's fields read in two columns on a wide screen. A form of four fields or more takes two columns, a long text (`textarea`, `wide: true`) the whole row, its buttons in a bar at the end, the submit last.
- **Then the finishing pass** (`convertigo://guides/ngx/design-finish`): once the pages work, `browser-verify` with `design: true` reviews them and says what to fix.
- **Consistency.** Hand-written rules read the tokens: spacing `--app-space-1` to `--app-space-6`, type `--app-font-size-xs` to `--app-font-size-2xl`, `--app-radius-sm`, `--app-radius`, `--app-radius-lg`, surfaces `--app-surface`, `--app-surface-2`, `--app-muted`, `--app-border`, `--app-primary-soft`, colors `--ion-color-<name>`, and the classes `app-card`, `app-page-header`, `app-subtitle`, `app-cell-num`. Never a fixed color or pixel size the theme already gives.
