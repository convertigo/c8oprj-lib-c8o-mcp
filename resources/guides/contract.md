# Convertigo Contract-First Delivery

Define the stable public business contract before wiring the UI. A stub proves wiring only; live transport proof is still required. Keep transport in typed transactions, mutate through MCP, verify backend and UI, then project-save. Never patch generated files.

## Drawers
- `convertigo://guides/contract/overview` — Scope, prerequisites and route selection.
- `convertigo://guides/contract/mandatory-workflow` — Mandatory workflow.
